# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Everyone is welcome to participate in projects maintained by
[@cravingmaker](https://github.com/cravingmaker), regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, experience, gender identity or expression, nationality,
appearance, race, religion, sexual orientation, or socioeconomic status. We
expect participants to treat one another with care, respect, and good faith.

## Expected behavior

- Engage kindly and honestly, and assume good intent.
- Keep feedback constructive and focused on the work.
- Respect different viewpoints, experiences, and communication styles.
- Respect personal boundaries and privacy.
- Credit others' contributions and the sources you build on.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and help repair harm.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is prohibited, as are threatening or encouraging it:

- **Harassment**, including continued contact after someone asks you to stop.
- **Personal attacks**: insulting, demeaning, or derogatory remarks about a
  person or group.
- **Discrimination**, including stereotyping people based on their identity.
- **Sexualized behavior** and unwelcome sexual attention.
- **Violating privacy**, such as sharing someone's private information without
  their permission.
- **Threats or incitement** of violence or self-harm.
- **Impersonation**, including using other accounts to evade moderation.
- **Spam**, including unsolicited promotion and bulk or low-effort automated
  content, such as unreviewed AI-generated issues, comments, or pull requests.

Retaliation against someone who raises a concern or participates in a review is
also prohibited.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to every repository owned by **@cravingmaker** that
doesn't provide its own. It covers issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews,
commits, and other project spaces. It also applies when someone officially
represents a project elsewhere.

Everyone on GitHub must also follow the
[GitHub Community Guidelines](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-community-guidelines).

## Reporting concerns

Report concerns privately to
**[cravingmaker.id@gmail.com](mailto:cravingmaker.id@gmail.com)**. Include relevant
links, screenshots, and any details you feel comfortable sharing. If you're
unsure whether something breaks this code, report it anyway. Keep sensitive
information out of public issues.

The project maintainer, **@cravingmaker**, handles reports, aims to acknowledge
them within 7 days, and limits disclosure to what is necessary to review and
address them.

If your concern involves the maintainer, or you'd rather not contact them
directly, report it to GitHub: use **Report content** on the comment, issue, or
pull request, or **Block or report** on the user's profile. See
[Reporting abuse or spam](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

To report a security vulnerability, follow the affected repository's security
policy instead.

## Responding to violations

The maintainer will consider the available evidence, severity, and any repeated
behavior, and may skip steps for serious incidents. Content that breaks this
code may be edited, hidden, or removed, and conversations may be locked.

1. **Warning**: a private, written warning that explains the problem and the
   expected behavior.
2. **Temporary restriction**: a warning plus a cooldown period without
   interaction in project spaces, for repeated or more serious violations.
3. **Temporary ban**: exclusion from all project spaces for a set time, with
   conditions for returning, for a pattern of violations or a serious incident.
4. **Permanent ban**: permanent exclusion from all project spaces. This is used
   rarely, when other steps have failed or when a violation is serious enough
   that the community can't be kept safe otherwise.

Where appropriate, the response will explain expectations, conditions for
returning, and how to repair harm, for example through an apology.
